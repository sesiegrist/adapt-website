title = "Postdoctoral fellowships"

description = "Two-year fellowships bridging two or more ADAPT laboratories"
+++

Two-year fellowships for early-career scientists leading integrative projects that bridge two or more ADAPT laboratories. Fellows are co-mentored by two faculty, follow a formal professional development plan, and take part in planning ADAPT workshops and symposia.

ADAPT brings together researchers from Biology, Chemistry, Environmental Sciences and Psychology to study how organisms, including humans, respond to environmental change.

Understanding how life responds to environmental change spans every scale of biology, from the regulation of genes inside a cell to the structure of whole ecosystems. No single laboratory or discipline covers that range. ADAPT is a collaboration incubator. It funds new teams that cross traditional fields, supports the postdoctoral and graduate researchers who carry the work, and builds a community of scientists at UVA and beyond who share the question.

The College of Arts and Sciences directs three field stations, Mountain Lake Biological Station, Blandy Experimental Farm and the Virginia Coastal Research Center, and holds research strengths from genomics and neuroimaging to remote sensing and ecosystem science. ADAPT connects them.

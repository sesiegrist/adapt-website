Social behavior affects health, immunity, disease spread, reproduction and lifespan, and it changes with age. This project asks how endocrine signaling acts through cells and metabolism to shape aging and, in turn, the social networks that animals form.

The work combines genetic tools in Drosophila, a newly sequenced genome and RNAi in the forked fungus beetle, and social network studies of beetle populations under natural conditions at Mountain Lake Biological Station.

Eastern forests remove a substantial share of carbon emissions, and the oaks that dominate them are in decline under drought and defoliation by the spongy moth. This project links the genes and alleles that influence how individual oaks respond to stress with remote sensing of outbreaks and demographic models of the forest carbon sink.

## Biological systems under pressure

How changing chemical and climatic conditions, novel pathogens and invasive species affect the functioning of organisms and ecosystems, including contagion, aging, social networks and resilience.

## Molecular pathways of adaptation

The genetic, epigenetic and biochemical mechanisms that allow organisms to adjust to fluctuating or extreme environments, and how those responses scale up to communities and ecosystems.

## Behavioral flexibility and decision making

How neural circuits, social interactions and ecological networks shape the behaviors organisms use to meet environmental change, and how those behaviors in turn shape communities.

## Resilience of biological systems

How organisms and ecosystems maintain function and recover from disturbance, and where the thresholds lie at which resilience gives way.
